Türkçe ses kayıtları, Khaled Moawad, Ammar Rashed ve Ekrem Çetinkaya tarafından sunulan Pocket TTS Turkish modeliyle oyun metinlerinden üretildi. Model CC BY 4.0 lisansı altındadır.

Voice sources

Turkish narration was generated from game text with Pocket TTS Turkish by Khaled Moawad, Ammar Rashed and Ekrem Çetinkaya, licensed under CC BY 4.0.

English narration was generated from game text with Kyutai’s Pocket TTS and the alba voice performed by Alba MacKenna. The sources are licensed under CC BY 4.0. Generated recordings were converted to MP3.
