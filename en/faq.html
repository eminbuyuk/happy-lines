FAQ for parents

About play records, family profiles, artwork and language settings.

Where are records stored?

Family profiles, artwork and play records stay on this device. The 7-day summary is also calculated only on this device. No child account is created; no learning or developmental assessment is made.

How do family profiles work?

Language, sound, the time limit and the full version are shared by every child on this device. Names are optional and stay on this device. Children switch by tapping the animal on the home screen.

How is play time counted?

Play time is stored only on this device and counts only while the game is on screen. Daily limits, breaks and bedtime settings are available in the full version. These limits do not apply to the free collection.

Do stars affect progress?

Stars reflect attempts: 3 with no mistakes, 2 with 1–2 mistakes, and 1 with more mistakes. Every completed level opens the next; stars never block progress.

What does the Progress section show?

These are calculated from play records on this device; they are not a skill or developmental assessment.

How can I share artwork?

Share PNG for an image, or use A4 PDF for printing. Detail lost in older small saves cannot be recovered. New paintings and scenes keep their source data.

What is the Memory Book?

A single A4 PDF from paintings and the things you did together. The file leaves this device only if you share it. The cover uses the name entered in the family profile. Each child’s book includes only artwork and notes from that child’s profile.

Does the language preview change the game language?

This choice changes only the word examples. Change the game language and speaking voice in language settings.

Do other language voices work offline?

Yes. Recorded voices for all five languages are inside the app; no internet or extra voice pack is needed.

How do I use Play Together?

Tap the question mark during a game for conversation prompts and screen-free ideas.
